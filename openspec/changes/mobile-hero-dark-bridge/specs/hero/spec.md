# Hero mobile black integration

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: isolated preview
The experiment MUST be a full-page independent temporary preview. It MUST NOT
change production, main preview, media bytes, backend or approved duration.

### Requirement: progressive black integration
The lower fade MUST be absent at initial progress and grow with native scroll.
The free doorway MUST become nearly black after opening, preserving door leaves,
frame, bars and handles. Reversing scroll MUST restore the original initial look.

### Requirement: stable layout
Facade, text and CTA MUST retain their position across height-only changes.
The dynamic wrapper MUST absorb extra height without scroll compensation,
touch interception or timed waits. Reduced motion/failure MUST retain a poster.
