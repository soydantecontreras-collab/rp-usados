# Visual-only vehicle patterns

The four labelled PNGs and three sample Jetta photographs exist solely for the isolated local Playground demo (`tools/start-demo-preview.ps1`). Every pattern states `QA LOCAL / NO ES STOCK`; the records also carry `DEMO VISUAL` names and use the noncommercial `TST` test currency. They are not part of the WordPress theme package or real RP Usados inventory. Replace all sample media with owner-supplied vehicle photographs before release.

The Jetta photos are free Unsplash photographs by Eugene Chystiakov, downloaded for this local design review under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license):

- `vento-side.jpg`: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-silver-car-parked-on-the-side-of-a-road-uXPyvuvivhE
- `vento-rear.jpg`: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-silver-car-parked-on-the-side-of-a-road-vn5GZczqaAc
- `vento-front.jpg`: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-white-car-parked-on-the-side-of-a-road-gxJsA0WGQgE

The photography is illustrative. Vehicle year, version, mileage, price and availability in this demo are fabricated test data, not facts about the photographed car or RP Usados stock.
